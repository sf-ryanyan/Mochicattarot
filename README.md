## 🌟 Overview

Mochi Cat Tarot is a full online tarot reading game. Every one of the 78 cards stars the same round white mochi cat, so the whole deck feels like one storybook. Pick what you want to ask about, shuffle the deck, cut it, choose your cards from a fan, and get a reading written just for your topic.

It is made for two kinds of players: **curious beginners** who want to learn what the cards mean, and **tarot fans** who want a real reading about love, career, or money.

> 💡 Free to play in your browser. No install, no sign-up. Switch between English and Chinese at any time.

## 🎮 How to Play

**1️⃣ Start · Tap the Crystal Ball**
On the home page, tap the glowing crystal ball with the mochi cat inside.

**2️⃣ Choose · Topic and Spread**
Pick what you want to ask about and how many cards to draw. Then keep your question in your mind. No typing needed.

**3️⃣ Shuffle · Mix the Deck**
Tap the big glowing **Shuffle** button and watch the mochi cat shuffle all 78 cards.

**4️⃣ Cut · Choose a Pile**
The deck splits into three piles of 26 cards. Pick the pile that calls to you. You will only draw from this pile.

**5️⃣ Pick · Choose From the Fan**
Your pile opens into a fan. Drag left or right to see more cards, then tap a card to pick it. Each pick flies into its place.

**6️⃣ Reveal · Watch the Cards Turn**
After your last pick, the cards flip over by themselves with a burst of stars.

**7️⃣ Read · Your Full Reading**
Read what each card means in its position, then the overall reading that ties everything together.

## 📜 Reading Rules

- 🃏 **Full deck.** Every reading uses all 78 cards.
- ✂️ **Cutting matters.** You only draw from the pile you choose.
- 🔒 **Picks are final.** Once a card is picked, it can't be changed, just like a real reading.
- ⚡ **Automatic reveal.** When your last card is picked, the reading starts on its own.
- 🌞 **Card of the Day.** One card per day on each device. A new card is ready tomorrow.

**🔄 Upright and Reversed**

| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⬆️ **Upright** | The card's energy flows freely |
| ⬇️ **Reversed** (about 1 in 3 cards) | The energy is blocked, too strong, or changing. Not "bad", just a gentle nudge to adjust |
