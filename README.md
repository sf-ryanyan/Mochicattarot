## 📌 Project Overview

Mochi Cat Tarot is a complete online tarot experience, built as a single web page.

Most free tarot sites fall into two groups. Some look dark and mystical, which can feel cold or even scary. Others look nice but give only one or two lines for each card. This project aims to do both well: a warm, friendly look that feels safe, and readings detailed enough to be truly useful.

It is designed for two kinds of users:

- **Curious beginners** who want to learn what tarot cards mean
- **Tarot fans** who want a full reading about love, career, or money

## ✨ Highlights

- **One character, one complete deck.** All 78 cards star the same round white mochi cat, so the deck feels like one storybook.
- **Detailed readings.** Every card has a picture description, upright and reversed meanings, love, career, and money readings, and practical action tips.
- **No typing needed.** Pick a topic, keep your question in mind, and start.
- **A real reading ritual.** Shuffle the deck, cut it into three piles, and pick cards from a fan, just like a reading at a real table.
- **Positions that fit the question.** In a three-card reading, the card positions change with the topic you choose.
- **Automatic reveal.** After the last card is picked, the cards turn over by themselves.
- **Smooth animations.** Shuffling, cutting, picking, and flipping all have their own effects.
- **Card of the Day, Card Library, and a beginner guide** for daily use and learning.
- **Fully bilingual.** Every page, card name, and reading is written in both English and Chinese.

### Art Direction

- **One consistent character.** Every cat uses the same body: a round mochi shape, an orange patch on the right ear, small dot eyes, pink cheeks, two paws, two feet, and a tail.
- **Numbers you can count.** On cards Ace to 10, the number of props matches the card number.
- **Court cards with their own costumes.** The Page wears a little beret, the Knight rides a wooden rocking horse, and the Queen and King sit on thrones with crowns.
- **Hard cards, drawn gently.** Difficult cards focus on change and hope. For example, on **Death** the cat waves goodbye to falling leaves while a new sprout grows, and on the **Ten of Swords** the sun rises behind the swords.

### What Every Reading Includes

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Tap the crystal ball** on the home page.
2. **Choose a topic:** General, Love, Career & Study, or Money.
3. **Choose a spread:** One Card, Three Cards, or Two Options.
4. **Keep your question in mind.** No typing needed.
5. **Shuffle.** Tap the glowing button and watch the mochi cat shuffle the deck.
6. **Cut the deck.** The deck splits into three piles. Pick the one that calls to you.
7. **Pick your cards.** Your pile opens into a fan. Drag left or right, or use the arrow buttons, to see more cards. Tap a card to pick it.
8. **Read your reading.** After the last pick, the cards flip over by themselves and the full reading appears.

## 📜 Rules

### Topics and Positions

In a three-card reading, the positions change with the topic.

| Topic | Card 1 | Card 2 | Card 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✨ **General** | Past | Present | Future |
| 💗 **Love** | You | Them | The Bond |
| 💼 **Career & Study** | Now | Obstacle | Advice |
| 💰 **Money** | Now | Opportunity | Advice |

### Spreads

| Spread | Cards | Positions | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One Card** | 1 | Guidance | A quick tip for right now |
| **Three Cards** | 3 | Based on the topic (see above) | Seeing the whole story |
| **Two Options** | 3 | Option A · Option B · Advice | Choosing between two paths |

### Drawing Rules

- **Full deck.** Every reading uses all 78 cards.
- **Cutting matters.** The deck is split into three piles of 26 cards. You only pick from the pile you choose.
- **Picks are final.** Once a card is picked, it cannot be changed, just like a real reading.
- **Upright or reversed.** About one in three cards comes out reversed (upside down). A reversed card is not "bad". It usually means the energy is blocked or changing.
- **Automatic reveal.** After the last pick, the reading starts on its own. There is no extra confirm button.
- **Card of the Day.** One card per day on each device. A new card is ready the next day.
- **Language.** Switch between English and Chinese at the top left at any time, even in the middle of a reading.

## 🧭 Design Decisions

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Cute mochi cat style** | Tarot can feel dark or scary. A soft, friendly character makes it welcoming for everyone. |
| **Pick a topic instead of typing** | Faster and easier, and the reading always matches the topic. |
| **Pre-written readings, no live AI** | Every reading is reviewed, consistent, and free to run. Nothing depends on an outside service. |
| **Shuffle, cut, and fan** | These steps copy a real reading. They make each draw feel personal and meaningful. |
| **Positions change with the topic** | "Past, present, future" doesn't fit every question. Love, career, and money each get their own positions. |
| **Gentle hard cards** | Cards like Death and The Tower are written as change and growth, never as warnings or threats. |
| **Reduced motion support** | If a device asks for less motion, the animations turn off and everything still works. |
| **No sign-up, no tracking** | Users can start right away, and no personal data is collected. |

## 🛠️ Tech Overview

- **One single HTML file.** No install, no build step, no server.
- **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** with no frameworks.
- **All 78 cards are drawn in code (SVG),** so they stay sharp at any size.
- **Hosted on GitHub Pages.**
- **Local storage only.** The browser saves just two things on the user's own device: the language choice and the Card of the Day.
- **Works on desktop, tablet, and phone.**
